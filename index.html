Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version: we can’t see your photos, videos, notes or files, and we don’t sell or share anything about you.

What we store

Your username, and your vaults in encrypted form. Everything inside a vault, including names, dates and album names, is encrypted on your device before it’s uploaded. We don’t have the keys, so we can’t read it.

Where it’s stored

Encrypted data is kept with our hosting provider, Supabase, which stores it for us and can’t read it either. It’s used only to sync your vaults between your devices and to let you share a vault when you choose to.

What we don’t collect

No email address, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking or analytics.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card or Apple ID details. Your iPhone checks with Apple which plan you have, and nothing about your purchases is sent to us.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This erases your account and every vault permanently. To stop paying, cancel your subscription in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Forgotten passwords

Because only you hold the keys, nobody, including us, can reset your password or recover your vaults without it, or without each vault’s recovery phrase.

Children

Velvault isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: velvaultaccs@outlook.com

Support

Need help? Email velvaultaccs@outlook.com and we’ll get back to you.

I forgot my password

Your data is encrypted with your password, so it can’t be reset. If you saved your sign-in details when you made your account, use those. A vault’s recovery phrase can still open that vault.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Velvault, go to the plans screen and tap Restore. Your plan belongs to the Apple ID that bought it.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Velvault → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account.
